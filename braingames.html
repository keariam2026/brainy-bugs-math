🌟 Why Play Brain Games?

Brain games give you a fun way to practice logical thinking, memory, concentration, and problem-solving. The more you practice, the more comfortable you can become with challenges.

🤔 Game 4: Brain Teasers

Riddle 1 🐥

Three birds sit on a tree. Two more birds join them. How many birds are there now?

Answer: 5 birds. 3 + 2 = 5!

Riddle 2 🍎

You have 6 apples and give 2 to your friend. How many apples do you have left?

Answer: 4 apples. 6 − 2 = 4!

Riddle 3 ✨

I am an odd number. Take away one letter from my name and I become even. What number am I?

Answer: Seven. Remove the letter "s" and "even" remains!

💚 Tips for a Stronger Brain

  • Practice a little every day.
  • Try solving a puzzle before looking at its answer.
  • Look carefully for number patterns.
  • Take breaks when you feel tired.
  • Celebrate your progress, even when a question is difficult!
