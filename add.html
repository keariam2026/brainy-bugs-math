Addition & Subtraction

Learn how to add numbers together and subtract one number from another with simple notes, colorful examples, and fun practice!

Learn • Practice • Improve

🌟 1. What Is Addition?

Addition means putting numbers together to find the total. The plus sign (+) is used for addition.

Example

You have 3 apples and get 2 more apples.

3 + 2 = 5 🍎

There are 5 apples altogether!

➕ Add Small Numbers

4 + 3 = 7

Count 4, then count 3 more.

🌷 Add Bigger Numbers

24 + 15 = 39

Add the ones, then the tens.

🩷 2. Addition With Carrying

When the sum of the ones is 10 or more, we carry the extra ten to the tens column.

Example: 27 + 15

Step 1: Add the ones: 7 + 5 = 12.

Write 2 in the ones place and carry 1 ten.

Step 2: Add the tens: 2 + 1 + 1 = 4.

Answer: 27 + 15 = 42

🌈 3. What Is Subtraction?

Subtraction means taking one number away from another to find the difference. The minus sign (−) is used for subtraction.

Example

You have 7 balloons and 3 balloons fly away.

7 − 3 = 4 🎈

You have 4 balloons left.

➖ Subtract Small Numbers

9 − 4 = 5

Start at 9 and count backward 4 steps.

🩵 Subtract Bigger Numbers

48 − 23 = 25

Subtract the ones, then the tens.

💜 4. Subtraction With Borrowing

If the top digit in the ones column is smaller than the bottom digit, we can regroup one ten as 10 ones.

Example: 42 − 17

Step 1: We cannot subtract 7 from 2 using whole positive digits.

Regroup 1 ten from 4 tens. Now there are 3 tens and 12 ones.

Step 2: Subtract the ones: 12 − 7 = 5.

Step 3: Subtract the tens: 3 − 1 = 2.

Answer: 42 − 17 = 25

📚 5. Important Math Words

Sum

The answer to an addition problem.

Example: 4 + 5 = 9

Difference

The answer to a subtraction problem.

Example: 9 − 5 = 4

Regrouping

Exchanging place value units, such as 1 ten for 10 ones.

💡 Tips to Remember

  • Addition combines quantities; subtraction takes away or finds the difference.
  • Line up digits according to place value when calculating vertically.
  • Remember to carry or regroup when needed.
  • Check subtraction by adding the difference to the number taken away.
  • Practice a few questions every day!
Remember: If 15 + 8 = 23, then 23 − 8 = 15. Addition and subtraction are related operations.
