💚 What Is a Pattern?

A pattern is something that repeats or follows a rule. Patterns can be made using numbers, shapes, colors, sounds, or objects. Once we find the rule, we can predict what comes next.

🌷 Part 1: Repeating Patterns

What Is a Repeating Pattern?

A repeating pattern is a sequence in which the same group of items repeats again and again.

To understand one, look for the smallest group that keeps repeating.

Example 1: Color Pattern

🔴 🔵 🔴 🔵 🔴 🔵

Rule: Red, blue, red, blue. The next color is red.

Example 2: Shape Pattern

Example 3: Three-Item Pattern

🩵 Part 2: Number Patterns

What Is a Number Pattern?

A number pattern is a sequence of numbers that follows a mathematical rule. The rule might be adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing by the same number each time.

Example 1: Counting by 2

2 → 4 → 6 → 8 → 10

Rule: Add 2 each time.

Example 2: Counting by 5

5 → 10 → 15 → 20 → 25

Rule: Add 5 each time.

Example 3: Decreasing Pattern

20 → 18 → 16 → 14 → 12

Rule: Subtract 2 each time.

🌈 Part 3: Growing Patterns

What Is a Growing Pattern?

A growing pattern changes by increasing according to a rule. Each new step might have more objects or a larger number than the previous step.

💜 Part 4: Find the Rule

A pattern rule tells us how the sequence changes. Look at the examples below.

Sequence Rule Next Number
2, 4, 6, 8 Add 2 10
5, 10, 15, 20 Add 5 25
30, 25, 20, 15 Subtract 5 10
3, 6, 12, 24 Multiply by 2 48
40, 20, 10 Divide by 2 5

⭐ How to Find a Pattern Rule

Step 1: Look carefully at the sequence.

Step 2: Compare one item with the next.

Step 3: Check whether you add, subtract, multiply, or divide.

Step 4: Use the rule to find the next item.
