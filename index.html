📚 Math Learning Notes

Review simple math ideas and learn something new!

🔢

Numbers

Learn the basics of numbers and counting.

What are numbers?

Numbers are symbols we use to count and describe quantities.

Examples:
1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6...

We can use numbers to count objects, compare amounts, and solve problems.
📐

Shapes

Discover common shapes and their properties.

Common Shapes

🔵 Circle — has no corners.
🟦 Square — has 4 equal sides.
🔺 Triangle — has 3 sides.
▭ Rectangle — has 4 sides.

Shapes are found everywhere around us.
➕

Addition & Subtraction

Understand two important basic math operations.

Addition

Addition means putting numbers together.

Example:
3 + 2 = 5

Subtraction

Subtraction means taking one number away from another.

Example:
7 - 3 = 4
📊

Patterns

Learn how numbers and shapes can follow rules.

What is a pattern?

A pattern is something that repeats or follows a special rule.

Example:
2, 4, 6, 8, 10...

The rule is:
Add 2 each time.
🧩

Math Puzzles

Use logic and problem-solving to find answers.

Problem Solving

Math puzzles help us think carefully and find solutions.

Try this:

If you have 3 apples and get 2 more, how many apples do you have?

Answer:
3 + 2 = 5 🍎

Train your brain with fun mathematical thinking.

Why play math games?

Math games can help improve:

• Memory
• Concentration
• Mental calculation
